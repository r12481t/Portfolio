Team project, paused

TAQEN Collection

A team of six built a print-on-demand clothing brand. It never got an order and is no longer developed. The case study covers what was built and what went wrong.

Built by
A team of six
Built with
HTML, JavaScript, Supabase, Netlify, Printify
Outcome
No orders. The store is still online, but no longer developed.
What this shows

A working storefront with a home page, product catalog, cart and information pages, backed by a database for products and orders. Ruhan Tahlil designed the website and created the initial designs. The case study also lists what went wrong and what the team learned.

Browser demo

Classly

Demo admin software for a language center: student registration, class tracking and course pricing. It runs in your browser and saves sample data on your own device. Designed with Barshon, who is not part of Curiohatch.

Built by
Ruhan Tahlil and Barshon. Design and systems design by Barshon.
Built with
HTML, JavaScript, browser localStorage
Outcome
A demo for an agency. It did not lead to a sale.
What this shows

A multi-screen app you can use yourself: a three-step registration form that works out fees, a class counter, a course and pricing list, staff accounts and an analytics overview. It is a demo with no server and no real sign-in.
