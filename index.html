How a project goes

  1. Audit

    You send your website address. We test the site and email you what we find.

  2. Proposal

    If you want help, we write down what we would change and what it would cost. Nothing starts without a written quote.

  3. Build

    We make the changes or build the new site. You see a preview on your phone and your computer before it goes live.

  4. Handover

    The finished site goes live, and you get the files and a short note on how to make small edits.

We do not promise rankings, leads or extra calls. A better website helps, but customers also come from reviews and referrals.

Questions people ask first

Is the audit really free?

Yes. You send your website address, we test it, and we email you what we find. You do not have to buy anything afterward.

What do you need from me?

A working website address and an email address to send the results to. Your type of business and where you are based help, but they are optional.

What if my website is fine?

Then we tell you so. We report what we find, including when the problem is small.

Will a new website bring me more calls?

We cannot promise that. A better website helps, but customers also come from reviews and referrals, so we do not promise rankings, leads or extra calls.

Is Curiohatch a company?

No. Curiohatch is a studio name used by Ruhan Tahlil, who does the audits, the fixes and the builds himself. It is not a registered company.
