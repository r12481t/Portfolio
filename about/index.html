About Curiohatch

Curiohatch is a small web studio. One person does the work: Ruhan Tahlil.

Who is responsible

When these pages say “we”, they mean the studio. Curiohatch is a studio name, not a registered company, and it is not an agency with a staff. Ruhan Tahlil runs it and does the audits, the fixes and the builds himself.

Ruhan is a front-end web developer. His own portfolio is separate from this site and shows his personal projects.

What we do today

Right now Curiohatch does three things for service businesses, especially plumbers and electricians: a free website audit, fixes for problems the audit finds, and new websites. The services page says what each one includes and what it does not.

We do not have client projects to show yet. The work page shows projects we built for ourselves, labelled for what they are.

Why we start with an audit

Most people choosing a web designer have to decide before they know what is actually wrong with their site. The audit comes first so you get something useful before you spend anything: a short list of what we found, in plain words. Then you decide. You can fix things yourself, ask us to quote fixes, ask about a new site, or do nothing.

How we work

  • We say in writing what we will do and what it costs before we start.
  • We report what we find, including when the problem is small.
  • We do not promise rankings, leads or extra calls. Customers also find tradespeople through reviews and referrals, and a better website supports that without replacing it.
  • You get the finished files and a short note on how to make small edits.

Other people on this site

Some projects on this site were made with other people, such as a friend who designed the Classly demo. Those people are not part of Curiohatch. Each keeps their own portfolio, and we link to a portfolio only when it exists and is current.

Videos

Separately, we make animated, story-based coding videos on Curiohatch on YouTube. A written lesson for each video will appear in the Learning library once the video is published. Planned courses are on the Courses page.
