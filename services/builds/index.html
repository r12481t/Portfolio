Websites for service businesses

A good trade website does a few jobs well: it says what you do, shows where you work, and makes it easy to call or ask for a quote from a phone.

What a site can include

  • A page for each service you offer
  • The areas you cover
  • A tap-to-call button
  • A quote request form
  • A layout designed for phones first, then checked on tablets and computers
  • Your license and insurance details, if you give them to us
  • Links to your real review profiles, such as your Google listing

What goes in depends on your business and on what can be kept up to date after launch. We tell you which parts we recommend and which we would leave out.

How a build goes

  1. You send a message

    Tell us what the business does, where it works and when you would like to be live.

  2. We send a written quote

    You get what we will build, what it costs and when it can be live, in writing.

  3. You see a preview

    We send a link you can open on your phone and your computer. You tell us what to change.

  4. Launch and handover

    The site goes live. You get all the files and a short note on how to make small edits.

What we need from you

  • Your business name and logo, if you have one
  • The text you want on the site, or notes we can write it from
  • Photos you own or have permission to use
  • Your phone number, opening hours and the areas you cover

Other options

Templates

If the budget is tight, we can fill one of our templates with your details. The layout stays as it is, so open it first and check you like it. See the landing page template

Online stores

We do not offer online stores as a standard package. If you need one, ask and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.
