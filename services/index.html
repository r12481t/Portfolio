Free website audit

We run your public website through Google’s PageSpeed test and our own checks, then email you a short, prioritized list of what to fix first. The audit is free, and you do not have to buy anything afterward.

Who it is for
Owners of a plumbing, electrical or other service business who already have a website and want to know what is wrong with it.
What it includes
  • Speed findings from PageSpeed, and what slows the page down
  • Mobile layout: readable text and buttons that are easy to tap
  • Broken links and navigation that leads nowhere
  • Basic search checks, such as page titles and descriptions
  • Basic accessibility checks, such as contrast, image descriptions and labels
  • A prioritized list of recommendations, sent as a short email
What it does not include
  • Security audits or penetration testing
  • Guaranteed search rankings
  • Legal or regulatory compliance certification
  • Formal accessibility certification
  • Guaranteed business results
  • An exhaustive review of every page
What we need from you
  • A working website address
  • An email address to send the results to
  • Your trade and your city or state (optional, but it helps)
How it starts
Fill in the contact form and choose “Free website audit”. After the audit, you choose what happens next. You can fix things yourself, ask us to quote fixes, ask about a new site, or do nothing.
How long it takes

We send the audit within 1 to 2 business days of receiving a request that has a working website address and a working email address. Business days are Monday to Friday, not counting US federal holidays. The clock starts on the next business day after your request arrives with everything we need. If something is missing, we write back, and the clock starts when we have it.

If we cannot meet the date, we will tell you before it passes and give you a new one. If we are at capacity, we will say so on this page instead of taking on audits we cannot finish on time.

Fixes

If the audit finds problems you want fixed, you tell us which ones. We quote each fix in writing first, you agree to the quote, and then we start.

Who it is for
Owners of a site they already have, who want specific problems repaired instead of starting over.
What it includes
  • A written quote for each fix before any work starts
  • The fixes you agree to
  • A new test after each fix, with the new results sent next to the old ones
What it does not include
  • Rebuilding a whole site. If a site needs more than repairs, we can talk about building a new one.
  • Promises about rankings, leads or extra calls
What we need from you
Your say-so on which problems to fix. We tell you what else we need when we write the quote.
How it starts
Ask through the contact form and choose “Fixes”, with your audit results if you have them.

New websites

We build websites for service businesses, especially plumbers and electricians. Depending on the project, a site can include service pages, the areas you cover, a tap-to-call button and a quote form, with a layout designed for phones first.

Who it is for
Trade businesses that need a better site, or have none, and want customers to see quickly what they do, where they work and how to call.
What it can include
  • A page for each service you offer
  • The areas you cover
  • A tap-to-call button and a quote request form
  • A layout designed for phones first, then checked on tablets and computers

What goes in depends on your business and on what can be kept up to date after launch. We tell you which parts we recommend and which we would leave out.

What it does not include
  • Online stores as a standard package. If you need one, ask and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.
  • Promises about rankings, leads or extra calls
What we need from you
Your business name and logo if you have one, the text you want on the site or notes we can write it from, photos you own or have permission to use, and your phone number, opening hours and areas you cover.
How it starts
You send a message, we send a written quote, you see a preview on your phone and your computer, then the site launches. How builds work, step by step

How pricing works

We do not publish a price list yet, and we do not show prices we cannot stand behind. Every fix and every new website is quoted in writing, per project.

What you get before you agree

A written quote that says what we will do, what it costs and when it can be done. Nothing starts until you say yes, and the audit is free either way.

What shapes a quote

  • Whether you are fixing a site you have or building a new one
  • How many pages you need
  • How much text and how many photos you can supply
  • Which features you want, such as a quote form

The quickest way to a real number is the free audit, then a short conversation about what you want.
