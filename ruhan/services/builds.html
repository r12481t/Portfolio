How a project runs

  1. Tell me what the business does, where it is and when you would like to be live.

  2. You get the package, any add-ons, the total and the delivery date in writing.

  3. The invoice comes through Payoneer to your email address. You open the link in it and pay online. Half is due before I start and half before the site goes live.

  4. I send a link you can open on your phone and your computer. You tell me what to change.

  5. The site goes live. You get all the files and a short note on how to make small edits.

what I need from you

  • Your business name and logo, if you have one
  • The text you want on the site, or notes I can write it from
  • Photos you own or have permission to use
  • Opening hours, address, phone number and email
  • Your prices, if you want them shown
