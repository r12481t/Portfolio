How a project runs

  1. You send me a message

    Tell me what the business does, where it is and when you would like to be live.

  2. I reply with a price and a date

    You get the package, any add-ons, the total and the delivery date in writing.

  3. I send an invoice

    The invoice comes through Payoneer to your email address and lists the ways you can pay. Half is due before I start and half before the site goes live.

  4. You see a preview

    I send a link you can open on your phone and your computer. You tell me what to change.

  5. Launch and handover

    The site goes live. You get all the files and a short note on how to make small edits.

What I need from you

  • Your business name and logo, if you have one
  • The text you want on the site, or notes I can write it from
  • Photos you own or have permission to use
  • Opening hours, address, phone number and email
  • Your prices, if you want them shown

Questions

Who owns the finished site?

You do. When the final payment is made I hand over every file, and you can move the site to any host you like.

Do you host the site?

No. I put it on a host in your name, so the account and the bill belong to you. Simple sites like these can often run on free hosting. A domain name usually costs $10 to $20 a year and you buy it from a registrar you choose.

What if I need changes after launch?

Small edits are covered by the 30-day add-on. For anything bigger, send me a message and I quote it before I start.

Can you build something that is not on this list?

Maybe. Send me a message that explains what you need. If I am not the right person for it, I will say so.

How fast do you reply?

By email, usually within one working day.

Tell me about your business.

Say what you sell or do, where you are, and the date you would like to be live. I reply with a price and a delivery date.
