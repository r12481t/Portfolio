What the test looks at

Speed
How long the page takes to appear on a phone and on a computer, and what slows it down.
Mobile layout
Whether text is readable and buttons are easy to tap on a small screen.
Broken links
Links and pages that end in "page not found".
Search basics
The page titles and descriptions Google reads when it lists your site.
Accessibility
Contrast, image descriptions and labels for visitors who use screen readers.
