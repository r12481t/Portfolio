Courses

Curiohatch makes animated, story-based coding videos on YouTube. Courses are planned as ordered paths through those lessons. No course is available yet.

What is here, and what is not

A course will group related lessons in a sensible order, with a stated audience, level and length. A course appears in the list below only when it is ready to use. A plan may be listed, but it is labelled as a plan, and no release date is promised.

This page has no sign-up, payment, progress tracking or certificates, because none of those exist.

No courses yet

No Curiohatch course exists yet, so there is nothing to enrol in and nothing to buy. When one is ready it will be listed here with what it covers, who it is for and how to start.

In the meantime, the Learning library collects the written lessons that go with each video.

Start with a lesson

Each video gets a written lesson in the Learning library, with the explanation, key ideas and links to related lessons. The videos are on the Curiohatch YouTube channel.
