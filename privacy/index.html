Privacy

What we collect when you contact us, what we do with it, and who else handles it.

Who we are

Curiohatch is a studio name used by Ruhan Tahlil. It is not a registered company. You can reach us through the contact form.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, email address, website address, trade, city and state, the kind of help you want, and any message you add. Only the name, email address and kind of help are required. The website address is required for a free audit.

How we use it

We use it to reply to you, to run and send your audit, and to prepare a quote if you ask for one. For an audit, we run your public website address through testing tools such as Google PageSpeed Insights. We do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

Who else handles it

The contact form is handled by Netlify, which also hosts this site. Netlify receives what you send, keeps a copy for us to read, and can pass it to our email inbox. Our email provider stores the messages we receive. Netlify may log technical details such as IP addresses, or set cookies, under its own policies.

Videos

Some Learning library pages hold a YouTube video. Nothing is requested from YouTube when the page loads. If you press Play, the video loads from YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com), and YouTube handles that request under its own policies. Each video also has a plain link to YouTube.

Analytics and cookies

Curiohatch uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits. Analytics cookies are used only if you allow it. The first time you visit, a small notice asks. Until you choose, the Google tag is on the page in a restricted mode (Google Consent Mode): it sets no cookies and sends only anonymous pings without a cookie identifier, which Google uses to estimate visits. As with any website that loads a Google script, your browser contacts Google’s servers, so Google can see your IP address. If you choose “No thanks”, the tag is switched off, any analytics cookies are removed, and the site works the same.

If you choose “Allow analytics”, Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_ followed by letters and numbers) and receives information such as the pages you view, your approximate location, your device and browser, and your IP address. Google handles that under its own policies. We use it to understand how the site is used. The site remembers your choice in your browser’s local storage. You can change it at any time with “Analytics settings” at the bottom of every page. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, the site treats that as “No thanks” and does not ask.

The site does not run advertising tools. Apart from the analytics cookies you allow, its own code sets no cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages for as long as we need them to answer you and to keep a record of what we agreed. To ask us to delete what you sent, or to see what we hold, send a message through the contact form and say so.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Last updated: October 19, 2026.
