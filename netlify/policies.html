Overview

We want you to love your Taqen order. Because our products are made on demand specifically for you, we handle returns and refunds on a case-by-case basis with your satisfaction as our priority.

Eligibility for Refunds

You may be eligible for a full refund or replacement if:

  • Your item arrived damaged or defective.
  • You received the wrong item or size.
  • Your order was lost in transit and not delivered within 30 days of the estimated delivery date.

We do not accept returns or issue refunds for:

  • Buyer's remorse or a change of mind.
  • Items that were ordered in the wrong size — please check our size guide before ordering.
  • Minor colour variations due to screen calibration differences.

How to Request a Refund

Contact us at support@taqen.store within 14 days of delivery with:

  • Your order number (found in your Gumroad receipt).
  • A clear photo of the item showing the issue.
  • A brief description of the problem.

We will respond within 2 business days and arrange a refund or replacement at no extra cost to you.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are issued back to your original payment method via Gumroad within 5–10 business days. Processing times may vary depending on your bank.

Print-on-Demand Fulfilment

All Taqen products are printed and fulfilled on demand by our production partner, Printify. Your order is created fresh when you place it — this means slightly longer production times but a more sustainable model with no overstock waste.

Production Time

Most orders enter production within 2–7 business days of purchase. During peak seasons (Ramadan, Eid, holiday periods), production may take a few extra days.

Estimated Delivery Times

  • United States: 5–10 business days after production.
  • United Kingdom & Europe: 5–12 business days after production.
  • Australia & New Zealand: 7–14 business days after production.
  • Rest of World: 10–20 business days after production.

These are estimates and not guarantees. Customs delays and local postal service conditions may affect delivery times.

Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are included in the product price displayed at checkout. The exact shipping fee is shown transparently on the Gumroad checkout page before you confirm payment.

Tracking

A tracking number is emailed to you once your order ships. Please allow 24–48 hours for tracking information to update in the carrier's system.

Customs & Duties

International orders may be subject to customs fees, import duties, or taxes levied by the destination country. These charges are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in our prices.
